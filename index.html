Why the old playbook stopped working

You’re not stuck because you’re not good enough. You’re stuck because the game changed, and no one told you.

The executives landing senior roles right now are not applying harder. They are not rewriting their resume one more time. They stop thinking about what they want and start thinking about what a specific buyer needs.

The hiring manager is not hiring the most qualified candidate. They are hiring to minimize risk and get to value as fast as possible.

That shift, from applying to strategically positioning, from volume to precision, and from candidate to solution, is what MissFit Coaching is built on.
