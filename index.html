Why the old playbook stopped working

You’re not stuck because you’re not good enough. You’re stuck because the game changed, and no one told you.

The executives landing senior roles right now are not applying harder. They are not rewriting their resume one more time. They are doing something most senior leaders never learn: they stop thinking about what they want and start thinking about what a specific buyer needs.

The hiring manager is not hiring the most qualified candidate. They are hiring to minimize risk and get to value as fast as possible. They want someone who has already solved the problem on their desk today, not someone who could figure it out.

That shift—from applying to strategically positioning, from volume to precision, and from candidate to solution—is what MissFit Mindset is built on.

I’ve been on the other side of this conversation

I know why strong candidates get passed over. Because I was the one doing the passing.

As a senior director at Motorola, acquired by Google, and vice president at Gallagher Consulting, I spent thirty years making the calls—who to hire, who to promote, and who to let go—across 26 countries and some of the most complex corporate environments in the world.

I understand how executive hiring decisions actually get made. Not how candidates imagine they are made.

I know what moves a decision-maker. I know what makes a strong candidate visible. And I know how to position an executive so they stop being one of many qualified candidates and start being the only obvious answer.

That is what MissFit Mindset does.

The MissFit Mindset Method

We don’t start with your resume. We start with who is buying.

The MissFit Mindset is a five-phase system. It begins with a diagnostic—an honest picture of where your search is breaking down and why. From there, we build your North Star: a clear, deliberate plan anchored in who actually needs what you bring and where your experience answers a problem that exists right now.

We then build the assets that take that strategy into the market: executive brand, LinkedIn profile, outreach strategy, and narrative. Not a resume rewrite. A go-to-market system.

When conversations start, we prepare you for every room—not as a candidate performing for an audience, but as an executive evaluating a partnership. We negotiate the offer. We make sure you land well.

The result: executives who work with MissFit Mindset land faster, land at the right level, and land in roles that use what they do best.
