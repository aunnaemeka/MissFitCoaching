About Dee

I spent three decades inside corporate hiring, at Motorola, through its acquisition by Google, at Gallagher Consulting, at Verizon, at Analyte Health, and across portfolio companies under New World Ventures. I sat in the room when the decision got made.

I started MissFit Coaching™ because I kept watching strong executives lose roles to people who were simply better positioned, and I knew exactly why, because I’d been the one making that call.

MissFit Coaching is the practice. MissFit Mindset™ is what I call the method itself, the philosophy behind every plan we build.

The MissFit Method™

Five phases. Each one earns the next.

  1. Phase One: The Diagnostic

    A specific read on where your search is breaking down, and why. It isn’t a personality assessment. It’s a direct answer about your situation.

  2. Phase Two: The Strategic Plan

    A clear, deliberate plan anchored in who needs what you bring, not a generic career summary or presentation.

  3. Phase Three: Market-Ready Positioning

    Building the assets that carry that strategy into the market: your executive brand, your narrative, your materials. It isn’t a resume rewrite. It’s your go-to-market system.

  4. Phase Four: Go-to-Market

    Conversations, outreach, momentum. You get prepared for every room, not as a candidate performing for an audience, but as an executive evaluating a partnership. When an offer comes, we build your negotiation strategy together, so you know exactly what to ask for and how to ask for it.

  5. Phase Five: Onboarding

    Support through the transition itself, so the first few months build on the same strategy that got you there.
